Buying Souvenirs

Sola skinte over de trange gatene i gamlebyen.
The sun shone down on the narrow streets of the old town.
Maria og Anna gikk langs små butikker fulle av fargerike skjerf, håndlagde lys og treskjæringer.
Maria and Anna walked past small shops full of colorful scarves, handmade candles, and wood carvings.
"Se på denne!" sa Maria og holdt opp en liten vikingfigur.
"Look at this!" said Maria, holding up a small Viking figurine.
"Den er perfekt for pappa."
"It's perfect for Dad."
Anna smilte.
Anna smiled.
"Ja, han liker sånne ting.
"Yes, he likes things like that.

How much does it cost?"

Maria turned the figurine over and found the price tag.

"199 kroner.

It's a little expensive, but it's handmade."

"You could ask for a discount," suggested Anna.

Maria nodded and walked over to the cash register.

"Hi!

I like this Viking figurine.

While the lady wrapped the figurine in gray paper with a red ribbon, Anna looked around.

"Yes, one with the Norwegian flag," replied Anna.

"It only costs 49 kroner."

Maria laughed.
"Det er mer min pris!"
"That's more like my price!"
De betalte og gikk ut igjen i sola, begge med en liten papirpose i hånden.
They paid and went back out into the sunshine, both with a small paper bag in their hands.
"Vi må ta et bilde her før vi drar," sa Anna.
"We have to take a picture here before we leave," said Anna.

"With our bags and the nice shop in the background."
"Ja!" svarte Maria.
"Yes!" replied Maria.
"Så har vi både suvenirer og minner."
"Then we'll have both souvenirs and memories."
